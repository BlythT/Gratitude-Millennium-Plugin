# Sound credits

Both sounds are from Freesound and released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain). They're the Freesound MP3 previews of the originals.

- `popper.mp3`: [Balloon Pop / Christmas cracker / Confetti Cannon](https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/458398/) by Breviceps
- `horn.mp3`: [Party horn.wav](https://freesound.org/people/audiosmedia/sounds/170583/) by audiosmedia

After changing a sound, run `node tools/embed-sounds.mjs` to rebuild `frontend/utils/partySoundData.ts`.
